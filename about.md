# Alternating Assist

Alternating is a technique for spamming faster and more consistently in Geometry Dash. **Alternating Assist** helps your alternating by auto-spamming clicks while you hold the jump button.

## How to use
1. Open the pause menu and press the green **AA** button.
2. Turn the assist **On** or **Off** with the checkbox.
3. Pick a stage: **1, 2, 3, 4 or Max**.
4. Hold your jump button in a level and the assist spams for you.

| Stage | Speed |
|-------|-------|
| 1 | ~8 clicks/sec |
| 2 | ~12 clicks/sec |
| 3 | ~16 clicks/sec |
| 4 | ~20 clicks/sec |
| Max | every frame |
